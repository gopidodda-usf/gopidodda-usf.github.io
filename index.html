Florida Poverty Trends

Overview

Florida Poverty Trends is a data analytics project that examines how poverty rates have evolved across Florida counties from 2012-2024, with a particular focus on Hillsborough County (Tampa) and its economic resilience over time.

The Challenge

Understanding regional poverty trends requires analyzing long-term data and comparing counties with diverse economic conditions, demographics, and recovery patterns.

The Solution

Using data from the U.S. Census Bureau, I developed a multi-year analytical dataset and interactive Tableau dashboards to evaluate poverty trends across Florida counties. The analysis revealed that Hillsborough County's poverty rate declined from 17.2% to 13.2%, demonstrating steady improvement and resilience through the COVID-19 period.

Socio-Economic Determinants of Crime

Overview

Socio-Economic Determinants of Crime is a data analytics project that explores how income, poverty, unemployment, and income inequality influence crime rates across U.S. states from 2010-2023.

Challenge

Crime is influenced by multiple socio-economic factors, making it difficult to identify which variables have the strongest impact and how changes in economic conditions affect public safety over time.

Solution

Using data from the FBI Crime Data API and U.S. Census Bureau, I built an analytics pipeline to collect, clean, and analyze state-level crime and economic data. Interactive Tableau dashboards revealed a $69,000 household income threshold beyond which crime rates stabilize, while highlighting the relationship between income inequality and violent crime.

Data Modeling

EV Adoption in Washington State

Overview

EV Adoption in Washington State is a data warehousing project that integrates EV registration, income, and policy datasets into a centralized analytical platform for studying electric vehicle adoption trends.

Challenge

EV adoption data is often fragmented across multiple sources, making it difficult to analyze the combined impact of economic, geographic, and policy factors.

Solution

I designed a STAR schema data warehouse and ETL pipeline to unify EV, income, and tax-exemption data from 2010-2024. SQL-based analysis revealed a strong correlation (0.68) between household income and EV adoption, while highlighting regional and manufacturer-specific adoption patterns.

Creating modular ambient music involves routing control voltage (CV) and audio signals through eurorack synthesizer modules to generate evolving textures, melodies, and soundscapes. By patching oscillators, filters, and delay modules together, the synth becomes a living, breathing sound instrument. It is essentially visual coding but with sound electricity. There are no presets in eurorack modular; every organic note, tone, and rhythmic pattern must be patched from scratch using physical cables, producing entirely unique audio experiences.
